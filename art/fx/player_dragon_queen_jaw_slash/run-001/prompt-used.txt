- effect_policy: all

Design goal:
The first version looked too decorative and too much like generic royal magic. This version is designed as a readable normal attack: two elegant sea-dragon fin blades snap shut like jaws, then burst at the target. It should not read as Dragon Queen Q's card-steal / collect-card special ability.

Frame plan:
1. Wind-up: two curved cyan-white fin blades open around a pearl target point.
2. Snap-in: the blades close into an X-like bite path.
3. Hit: bright pearl break point, jagged cyan-white impact and coral sparks.
4. Release: blade fragments pass through and dissolve.

Trigger design:
Use this as the normal adventure damage attack FX for Dragon Queen Q, not as her special ability FX.

Recommended runtime trigger:
1. When adventure mode calls PlayAdventureScoreAttackSequenceAsync and the attacker is the adventure enemy Dragon Queen Q, route PlayAdventureAvatarAttackAsync into a DragonQueen-specific attack branch.
2. Start CharacterAvatar.AvatarAction.Attack immediately on the Dragon Queen avatar.
3. Spawn this FX as a Sprite2D at the target avatar center, above both avatars, using frames fx-1..fx-4.
4. Play frame 1 at t=0.00, frame 2 at t=0.10, frame 3 at t=0.20, frame 4 at t=0.32.
5. Invoke onHit exactly on frame 3, around t=0.20, so score/damage resolves on the visible bite impact.
6. Fade and free the sprite after frame 4, around t=0.44.

Important separation:
- Normal attack trigger: PlayAdventureAvatarAttackAsync -> Dragon Queen branch -> this jaw-slash FX -> onHit on frame 3.
- Special ability trigger: ApplyDragonQueenAndKingAfterEnemyCaptureAsync -> StealRandomMainPlayerHandCardForEnemyAsync -> PlayEnemySpecialAbilityAvatarFxAsync -> ResolveQueenEffectAsync. Do not reuse this normal attack FX there.
