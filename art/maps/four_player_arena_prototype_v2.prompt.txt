Create one refined game UI prototype image for a top-down multiplayer card/strategy battle arena.

Pipeline intent from generate2dmap:
- map_mode: baked_scene_mode for visual review only
- visual_model: baked_raster
- runtime_object_model: none for this first review image
- collision_model: none
- engine_target: Godot project-native later, not connected in this version

Canvas and composition:
- 16:9 horizontal game screen, 1920x1080 feel.
- Center: large rectangular tabletop arena, about 58% of screen width and 55% of screen height, clean empty play surface for future gameplay.
- Four directional player zones around the center: top, bottom, left, right.
- Top and bottom player zones are horizontal compact panels. Left and right player zones are vertical compact panels.
- The layout supports 2, 3, or 4 players. Show all four seats as available, but keep inactive-looking seats restrained.
- Each player zone includes simple placeholders for an avatar frame, card slots, small resource counters, and a status/turn control shape.
- Keep the center uncluttered, with subtle grid/cloth/stone patterning and refined corner ornaments.

Style target:
- Pixel art with mature anime-inspired game UI, restrained fantasy board-game mood.
- Less cartoony than a mobile kids game; more like a polished tactical card battler prototype.
- Bright enough and welcoming, but calmer and more grounded.
- Use muted natural materials: warm parchment, light wood, carved stone, brushed brass, soft linen.
- Use four subdued accent colors for seats: desaturated blue, muted red, sage green, ochre gold.
- Thin crisp pixel outlines, moderate contrast, compact UI, balanced negative space.
- Background can suggest a calm fantasy garden or academy courtyard, but it must stay secondary and not steal attention from the board.

Reduce cartooniness:
- No oversized hearts, no toy-like glossy buttons, no exaggerated gem icons, no big wing decorations.
- Avoid candy colors, thick white outlines, plastic shine, chibi proportions, childish iconography, or loud saturation.
- Avoid busy waterfalls, giant castles, dramatic fantasy scenery, heavy ornamentation, and cluttered edge decoration.

Important constraints:
- No readable text, no labels, no numbers, no letters, no watermarks, no logos.
- Do not include player characters, monsters, enemies, or gameplay pieces yet.
- Do not fill the center arena with props; keep it ready for gameplay.
- Avoid tiny illegible UI clutter.
