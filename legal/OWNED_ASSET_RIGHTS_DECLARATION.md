# Deckland Owned Asset Rights Declaration

Declaration ID: `OWNER_DECLARATION_2026-08-13`

Declaration date: 2026-08-13

Declarant: Deckland project owner/publisher of record

## Declaration

The project owner/publisher of record confirms that all Deckland-specific
visual art, animation, music, sound effects, voice/audio, and video content
identified as `project_owned` in `ASSET_RIGHTS_REGISTER.csv` is owned or
otherwise controlled by the project with the rights required to use, modify,
reproduce, market, commercially distribute, and sublicense it as part of the
game and related promotional materials.

Some project-owned assets were generated or assisted by artificial
intelligence tools. The project owner/publisher of record confirms that the
applicable tool or service terms and the manner of use permit the intended
commercial use and distribution. Because only some assets involved AI, and
no reliable per-file provenance was supplied, the register deliberately uses
`original_or_ai_assisted` instead of guessing which individual files did.

This declaration does not claim ownership of third-party software, fonts,
trademarks, or bundled plugin content. Those items are separately identified
in `THIRD_PARTY_COMPONENTS.csv` or in the third-party rows of
`ASSET_RIGHTS_REGISTER.csv` and remain subject to their respective licenses
and terms.

## Scope and limitations

- The declaration covers the files listed by path and SHA-256 in the asset
  register at the time it is generated.
- A changed file hash is a new revision and must be reviewed through the
  register update process before release.
- Project logos, character names, product names, and other brand elements may
  require separate trademark review; this declaration records copyright and
  contractual usage rights only.
- This record contains no fabricated personal name or signature. The actual
  legal publisher should retain any signed declaration and supporting evidence
  in its private corporate records.

## Release confirmation

Before a release candidate is approved, the publisher should confirm that
this declaration remains accurate, review any new or replaced assets, and
retain supporting source files and commercial-term evidence outside the
public game package.
