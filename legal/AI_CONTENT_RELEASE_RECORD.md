# Deckland AI Content Release Record

Record date: 2026-08-13

Status: internal release record; excluded from public packages

## Confirmed facts

- Some Deckland-specific image, audio, or other media assets were generated or
  assisted by artificial intelligence tools before being included in the
  game.
- The project owner/publisher of record confirms that the applicable terms
  permit commercial use and distribution of those outputs in Deckland.
- No individual file is labelled as AI-generated unless reliable per-file
  provenance exists. The asset register therefore uses the conservative
  category `original_or_ai_assisted` for project-owned media.
- The repository review found no feature intended to generate AI content for
  players while the game is running. If such a feature is added, this record
  and the platform disclosure must be reviewed again.

## Steamworks release action

Before submitting the store build, the publisher must complete the current
Steamworks Content Survey truthfully. Deckland should disclose its applicable
pre-generated AI content there. Commercial permission does not replace this
platform disclosure requirement.

The survey response should describe the actual asset categories and tools
used. Tool names, account terms, model/version information, dates, prompts,
and generation/export records were not supplied for this repository record
and must not be invented. Retain the real evidence in a private archive.

> Some visual and/or audio assets included in Deckland were created or refined
> with generative AI tools before release. The developer reviewed the
> applicable service terms and confirms that the outputs may be used and

> categories used before submission.]
